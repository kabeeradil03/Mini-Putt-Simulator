This repository was a combined effort that I (Kabeer Adil) led in my third year of computing. Our task was to create a C++ GUI to reflect a certain task or application. As gamers and gamemakers, we decided to pursue creating a basic, top-down mini-golf simulator to meet the requirements. We used workflow tools such as Jira/Confluence/Bitbucket to manage deadlines and ensure work was proceeding at a decent pace. Personal responsibilities included asset acquisition, collision physics, preliminary research into GUI and SFML code boilerplates and of course, managing and delegating tasks for a team of 8 agile programmers. It was a fun and fulfilling exercise that taught me leadership and gave me an intimate look at game-making.
